**Version 1.0 · 2026-08-09 · Prepared materials; ALL external submissions are founder
actions (account creation and form submission are not performed by the system).**

## A. Neudata listing (free; highest-leverage free action)
**Category:** attention/trend measurement with audited outcome ledgers (position AWAY
from "social sentiment" — see the incorporation doc's category push-back).
**One-line:** *The attention dataset a compliance team can actually approve and a
quant can actually reproduce — point-in-time from the start, lineage documented,
rights written down, absences shown honestly.*
**Listing description (short):** NowTrendIn measures where human attention is moving
before it arrives — topic-level attention gradients from disclosed public/licensed
sources, instrument-level money-flow reads, and (the differentiator) THREE held-out,
never-deleted accuracy ledgers that score every detection against external ground
truth (Google-breakout timing; realized price direction) under pre-declared,
version-stamped rules. Scores ship WITH their components and vintages; rates the
evidence doesn't support are withheld with the threshold stated.
**What Neudata will ask → where it lives:** data dictionary (`DATA_DICTIONARY.md`) ·
history depth (365-day vintage panel + ledgers) · methodology (published Methodology
page + lineage statement) · rights (`RIGHTS_REGISTER.md`) · PII/MNPI policies ·
company background/permanency (single founder, disclosed; DDQ gate 9) · pricing
(bands TBD; evaluation via trial template).
**Known check-item risks, pre-answered:** permanency (disclosed + runbooks);
coverage (per-leg, `/diag/coverage`; attention leg reported in topics); the
provisional flag on the attention rate (disclosed as discipline, not weakness).

## B. BattleFin Data Quality Scorecard — documentation to their published spec
- **Metadata summary:** the data dictionary + update cadences + identifier posture.
- **Univariate:** per-field distributions computable from the vintage panel
  (component score distributions; ledger lead-time distribution — served live).
- **Bivariate:** component-vs-outcome reads live in the ledgers (LED vs component
  levels — the feature-mining audit exists in `audits/ledger-research/`).
- **Time-series:** the 365-day per-cycle panel with `scored_at` stamps; coverage
  by month via `/diag/coverage`.
- **Completeness:** honest-absence design — absences are declared (`null`/`absent`),
  never imputed; enrollment completeness itself is published (69.6% as of
  2026-08-09).

## D. Cloud-marketplace evaluation (Snowflake et al.)
**Verdict: DEFER.** The mechanics are attractive (buyers pay from existing committed
spend — procurement friction removed), but listing requires delivery engineering
(shareable tables, refresh automation) that Phase 1 has not built; revisit after the
first direct evaluations prove the extract shape buyers actually want.

## E. Sequencing doctrine (adopted)
Do NOT open with mega-fund data desks. First tier: smaller funds, family offices,
RIAs, corporate strategy/IR, sell-side research — buyers of processed, reproducible
signals. Publish the methodology and the QUESTION the product answers, precisely —
~80% of dataset discovery is thesis-driven search, not vendor prospecting. Expect
the first evaluation gate to be documentation-heavy (it is now pre-built) and
retention after adoption to be the durable economics (>90% industry retention).
