## What D measures (the instrument, not the poetry)

First-timer influx and engagement asymmetry on the **author-bearing public platforms we
collect**, routed by `platform_tier`. Under `D_PLUMBING_V2` (built 2026-08-20, flip
pending): denominator = author-bearing signals only; community-age guard active; blog-lane
rows carry real first-timer bits going forward.

## What D structurally CANNOT see (the exclusion boundary, stated as doctrine)

1. **Closed/private platforms** — Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, group chats, DMs.
   No collection exists or is planned; any "private conversation" language in older docs
   is aspiration, not measurement (de-poeticized 2026-08-20).
2. **Authorless surfaces** — all RSS/news wires, Google Trends, Wikipedia, socialcrawl,
   firecrawl. These carry no author identity, so first-timer semantics do not exist for
   them. Their leading signals are the HELD-OUT authorless indicators
   (`darkmatter_indicators.py`: venue first-coverage, incumbent displacement, breadth
   velocity, engagement divergence) — shadow-trial candidates, not yet score inputs.
3. **Retired/uncollected communities** — **Reddit: FORMALLY RETIRED 2026-08-20** (Chairman:
   no API key will be obtained). X: depth-capped (4,800 posts/mo, movers-only). Guardian
   API: on hold. The author-bearing universe is **GitHub, HackerNews, bluesky, lemmy**
   (+ devto/hashnode/discourse/wordpress/blogger/medium/ghost under D_PLUMBING_V2).
4. **Non-Latin-script text** — the 2026-08-20 unicode fixes extended coverage to
   Latin-with-diacritics, Greek, and Cyrillic. **CJK, Arabic, Hebrew, Thai, Devanagari
   remain outside the boundary**: the entity extractors anchor on capitalization, which
   these scripts do not have (and German's all-noun capitalization over-fires). A null on
   these languages is absence of instrument, never absence of attention.
5. **Domains without wired supply** — D coverage is ~90% technology by volume
   (2026-08-20 census). The 08-20 expansion (sports desks, 12 new domains on general
   platforms) begins correcting this; until a domain's supply matures past §16a
   calibration, its D reads carry `d_measured` honestly and prove nothing either way.

## The standing interpretation rule

**A topic with `d_measured = 0` (or NULL, pre-epoch) has an UNREADABLE D — its stored 0
is "could not read", never "read quiet."** The backtest census read ~32.4% of the working set in this state — but that
figure was computed from PLATFORM MEMBERSHIP, not author resolution, so it is an
**UNDERCOUNT** (board 2026-08-20 evening). It is corrected to an author-resolution basis
after the next census; until then cite it as a floor, not a measurement. Any D-based claim carries this scope or it
overclaims. This is §15a / A3 pointed at our own component.
