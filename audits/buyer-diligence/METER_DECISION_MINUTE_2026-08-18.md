escrow/backup-calculation-agent path is rejected. The model: license our API;

record. (Board caution preserved on the record, not re-litigated: the collation

the product is proprietary AGENT capability over proprietary data, a category the
comparables did not have. The sealed ledger, PIT store (b), and live record (c)
remain the evidence base under this structure unchanged.)
