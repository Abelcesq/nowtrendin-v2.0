## STRONG CONVERGENCES (6+ seats independently)

1. **No attention rate is citable today — 9/9, and the v1-epoch wins are quarantined as
   non-evidence.** The Statistician sharpened it: tracked-race 27.3% (N=55) is
   significantly BELOW the 50% null (exact binomial p≈0.001) — the evidence favors
   losing races. The Forecaster's framing: the 15 LED wins were resolved on queries
   sealed *after* the fact — "UNSCORABLE as a track record"; backfill referee checks are
   audits, not scores, and carry a permanent "post-hoc referee, query unsealed" marking.
   **Converged citation bar:** ≥50 sealed new-epoch races · beating the 50% null at the
   Bonferroni-corrected threshold · referee corroboration > 0 (Forecaster adds: majority
   corroboration + non-degenerate Brier skill; Operator adds: unambiguous queries).
2. **What is honestly sellable TODAY is the apparatus, not a rate (8/9):** a sealed,
   held-out, never-deleted, independently-refereed ledger that publishes its own losing
   scorecard — plus the one established coverage fact: **raced 12/12 top-decile surges,
   0 pre-broken** (Statistician: CI lower bound ~73.5% — the only Grade-B claim in the
   pack). The Outsider's point-blank: "we do not yet have a citable hit rate; here is
   the measurement apparatus" is the only honest DDQ answer.
3. **"0 false positives" is a censoring artifact and must be caveated/stripped
   everywhere it renders (Challenger, Statistician, Forecaster, Operator):** under the
   365-day patience window no FALSE_POSITIVE can structurally exist before ~mid-2027,
   so the figure is smoothness Thorp's rule forbids in marketing. Corollary
   (Forecaster): every rate published before the first timeout wave is structurally
   biased UPWARD — interim-rate inflation must be disclosed.
4. **Item C is the board's consensus best work** — mechanism traced to the exact lines,
   durable diagnosis, unanimously approved. But three seats bound the M0 *table*:
   retention-erosion confound (Challenger), no rank test + zero-rate/median
   disagreement + overlapping-pool "replication" (Statistician). The decision stands on
   the CODE; the table is corroborative and not externally citable.
5. **The D-early trial ships DARK first, and the levers flip SEPARATELY (7/9).** The
   design's "one assessed package" was rejected by six seats as attribution-destroying
   (factorial arms or staggered flips ≥30d; Executioner; Statistician's power note: at
   N=50, α≈0.007 needs ~33/50 = 66% to clear — failure to clear is uninformative, not a
   kill). H16's "inversion flips" criterion is a **manipulation check, never evidence
   of skill** (Statistician; Lever A raises race-cohort D by construction).
6. **`d_at_enroll` stamping is prerequisite #0** (Executioner — it exists nowhere in

## UNIQUE HIGH-VALUE FINDINGS (single seats)

- **Executioner — Lever B as designed cannot work:** a rescore "+2h/+6h AFTER
  enrollment" moves nothing the ledger judges (detection_date is stamped and sealed at
  enrollment). The value is PRE-enrollment: fast-lane near-crossers so they enroll
  earlier. Also: `fastlane_recheck.py` (D10) already exists, flag OFF, free-source by
  construction — the build target is a rewrite of its trigger, not a new module. X
  budget pause = confounded arm; start the 90-day clock at the 09-01 reset.
- **Operator — Wikipedia common-mode:** the design lists Wikipedia in the fast-lane
  rescore set while Wikipedia is the REFEREE. Remove it from the rescore set (or mark
  such rows unauditable) — otherwise referee independence dies by wiring. Also: TCI
  discipline — plan how PROVISIONAL is *retired* (pre-committed, published criteria) or
  its removal reads as a marketing flip; trust damage lands after performance recovers.
- **Expansionist — the referee is parochial:** `referee_wikipedia.py` hard-codes
  en.wikipedia while several LED wins are non-English — 0/15 partly measures the
  referee's language ceiling. Multilingual resolution is a condition before any
  "referee-checked" claim in sales material. Also: `fastlane_recheck.py` and discovery
  hard-code `geo=US`; every validator (referee, sweep, trending seed) is single-locale
  while the detection architecture is locale-free — "we can detect a non-English trend
  but can never prove it."
- **Challenger — composition-shift attack on the flip:** post-flip, D-early changes WHO
  crosses the enrollment floor, so "new-epoch rate beats the null" can be manufactured
  by enrolling easier races. Enrollment admission must be computed D-early-free (or
  stratified). Also: the 50% null itself needs a written derivation (sweep cadence vs
  Google granularity biases SAME_DAY assignment).
- **Forecaster — two seal defects:** (1) fail-open articles seal as NULL and fall back
  to hindsight opensearch — serve `referee_sealed: false` on such rows; (2) stamp
  `query_ambiguous` at ENROLLMENT, not only at sweep. Brier: `skill_vs_base_rate` is
  arithmetically forced negative at n=0 negatives — serve `degenerate: true` and
  suppress the skill field while single-class.
- **Economist — the null is too easy:** the true Malkiel baseline is a naive momentum
  comparator (yesterday's risers), likely harder than the coin flip; and no
  cost-per-LED economics exist (a lead is only sellable if repeatable per dollar).
- **Outsider — two DDQ instant-fails outside the pack:** vendor permanency (bus-factor
  one, undisclosed/unmitigated) and no data-rights/PII register anywhere. Also demands
  the seal be enforced by schema/trigger, not discipline. Plain-English rule: "Dark
  Matter"/"LED"/"pre-broken" must translate in any DDQ answer.
- **Guardian — the universe statement is missing:** the product never states its
  exclusion boundary as a whole (closed platforms — TikTok/Discord/private communities —
  where attention now originates are structurally unmeasurable); and the referee's own
  boundary (encyclopedic attention; blind to commerce/dev-tool races) reframes 0/15 as
  "unproven under a conservative referee," not "disproven."
