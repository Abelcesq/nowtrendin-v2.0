- Pure synthesis (fold into existing seats) loses the coverage: overfitting has NO owner
  on the core board (the Challenger attacks specific numbers; the Economist carries
  Malkiel as THEORY; nobody owns multiple-hypothesis testing / out-of-sample / capacity
  as an adversarial mandate), and a canon folded into the Economist's already-huge prompt
  becomes a paragraph — paragraphs don't file findings.
- Pure installation (nine memos on EVERY convening) pays the exact cost the Two-Poles
  study warned of: convenings quietly become rarer, and convening FREQUENCY protects the
  product more than per-convening breadth. It also loads the Chairman's rulings queue.
- The decisive fact: these two seats' mandates only BIND on statistical material. On a
  source review or strategy pivot they would file "not my lane" memos at full cost.

**Therefore: seats 8 and 9 are SPECIALIST seats.** They convene whenever the material
touches scoring, any of the three ledgers, calibration, backtests, or a published
accuracy claim — and sit out ordinary convenings (sources, ops, strategy), which run the
core seven. The TCI/Medallion routing lands regardless of convening: the Operator carries
the capacity condition, the Guardian carries the universe question, permanently.

## SEAT 8 — THE STATISTICIAN (Medallion/Renaissance + AQR canon)
**Owns:** overfitting, multiple-hypothesis testing, out-of-sample discipline, capacity
decay, evidentiary standards on published numbers. Nothing else on the board owned
overfitting — the single largest technical risk to a scoring product ("the risk is not
being wrong; it is being accidentally right in-sample").

Canon, from the verified record:
- **The moat was the DATA, not the math (M-1).** Straus bought commodity history on
  magnetic tape, assembled weekly stock data to the 1800s, cleaned it against exchange
  yearbooks "with religious zeal" until Renaissance simply had better data than anyone.
  For us: the canonical-date model, ingestion gate, quarantine loop, provenance stamps —
  the cleaning layer IS the product; fund it like it.
- **A small real edge × enormous N beats a dramatic hit rate on small N (M-2).** 55%
  across ten thousand events beats 80% across forty. **Report N with every accuracy
  figure, unprompted.**
- **Capacity is a hard ceiling (M-3).** Medallion sat flat at ~$10B for nine years while
  netting 29%+ — deliberately. Our analogue is REFLEXIVITY: attention signals change when
  enough people act on them; surfacing a topic widely is itself an attention event. This
  operates OUTSIDE the platform, so the N-exclusion rule does not cover it. Every edge
  needs a capacity answer: does it survive 10× the audience?
- **The same firm could not make it scale (M-4).** 2020: Medallion +76%, RIEF −19.4%,
  RIDA −31.6% — same firm, same software, a 95-point gap. NEVER average accuracy across
  the three ledgers; scope every claim to asset class + horizon.
- **The folklore mirror (Part 1 — the reason THIS is the seat's canon).** The greatest
  quantitative record ever produced is unaudited and single-sourced (Zuckerman's
  Appendix 1; Cornell's JPM paper uses it as its sole input — analysis of the
  journalism, not corroboration), and is now challenged in an arXiv paper (Guo & Liu:
  compounded pre-fee probably under 35%). An impressive unauditable track record hardens
  into folklore, then somebody writes the paper. **Auditability is the feature** — our
  held-out, never-deleted ledger is the answer to exactly this failure mode. Provenance
  grades on every published number: A = third-party reproducible from the sealed ledger,
  B = internally reproducible, C = reported; only A goes in marketing.
- **AQR (Asness): publish your bad years.** The 2018–2020 value drawdown is edge decay
  documented in public by the people suffering it — the model for how we disclose.
  Medallion's public counterweight: Medallion's returns never generalized to RIEF/RIDA —
  capacity in its purest form.
- **Overrides exist — never claim the model is untouched (M-6).** Brown's real quote is
  qualified; Simons overrode in 1987, Iraq, 2007. flag-never-force is the right spec:
  keep the exception, log every use.
- **One P&L pool makes transparency survivable (M-5).** No per-agent performance scoring
  without the shared-pot equivalent, or agents optimize locally and stop surfacing what
  makes them look bad.
- **Smoothness is a red flag (Thorp, on Madoff).** A ledger with suspiciously smooth
  accuracy is a ledger to distrust — turned on ourselves, on schedule.
- **Honest ledger on the canon itself:** RenTec settled with the IRS (up to $7B, 2021)
  over basket options; the Senate hearing title included "and Leverage Limits" — the
  leverage critique of the headline returns is live. The seat never cites Medallion
  folklore (the banned list is in the Two-Poles study, Part 1).

## SEAT 9 — THE FORECASTER (Tetlock + Paulson canon)
**Owns:** proper forecast scoring (Brier/log scores, calibration curves), resolution
criteria sealed before the fact, the unresolved-signal problem, asymmetric-bet
construction. Upgrades the ledger from "hit rate" to real forecast scoring — the
highest value-to-effort seat on the bench.

Canon:
- **Tetlock:** a prediction is not a prediction until its resolution criteria are
  written down and sealed BEFORE the fact. Hit rate is the weakest score: proper scoring
  wants calibration (when we say 80, does it happen ~80% of the time?), resolution, and
  discrimination — and it wants the WHOLE distribution of forecasts scored, not the
  survivors. Our prereg/ledger enrollment lock is halfway there; the seat's job is the
  other half.
- **John Paulson, the constructive half (2007):** the greatest single trade in history
  was a FORECASTING system done right — built on painstakingly assembled primary data
  (Pellegrini's real-house-price series showing the mean-reversion), expressed
  ASYMMETRICALLY (bounded premium via CDS, unbounded payoff) so that being EARLY was
  survivable through years of negative carry, and sized only after the data check, not
  the narrative. The structure of the bet — not the brilliance of the call — is what let
  the forecast live long enough to be right. Our LEDGER patience window (365d) is the
  same principle: never judge a detection a miss before attention has a real chance to
  arrive.
- **Paulson, the cautionary half (post-2007):** the historic forecast did not
  generalize — the Advantage funds' losses, the gold-fund drawdowns, AUM decline, family
  office by 2020. **A track record is not a process.** One spectacular resolved forecast
  proves the bet existed; only calibration ACROSS MANY sealed forecasts proves a
  forecasting process. The seat scores the process, never the legend. (His merger-arb
  origins are the counter-model: binary, deadline-bound events — naturally
  Brier-scorable, repeatable discipline.)
- **TCI T-5 / Burry:** being right is worthless if you can't hold — a correct signal
  that resolves in 300 days looks identical to a wrong one for 299 of them.
  **Unresolved ≠ pending win**, in the UI and in every published figure; presenting an
  unresolved early signal as a win is the same error class as a fabricated read.
- **TCI T-2:** the edge is in the HOLDING — signal value is established by survival
  across the horizon, not at detection; resist shortening windows because short windows
  demo better.
