  READING THE PERCENTILES
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Each percentile is that primitive's rank against ITS OWN prior
  history, computed only from data knowable on the as-of date.
  A high reading means 'this condition is unusual by its own
  standard'. It does not mean anything is about to happen.

  WHAT THIS PANEL DOES NOT SAY
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * It does not forecast a crash, or a date, or a probability.
  * It does not estimate a return, and no weight here was fitted
    to returns.
  * It is not investment advice and implies no position.
  * The crash-time parameter t_c is deliberately not estimated;
    the replication literature finds it poorly determined.

==============================================================================
  N MARKET SCORE  --  METHODOLOGICAL FOUNDATIONS
==============================================================================

  OBJECTIVE   Conceptual & mathematical foundations
  SOURCE      Mandelbrot & Hudson, The (Mis)Behavior of Markets (2004)
  CONTRIBUTES Fat tails, volatility clustering, multifractal scaling. Why Gaussian risk measures understate danger.
  PRIMITIVE   TURBULENCE
  LIMIT       Descriptive, not predictive. Mandelbrot argued markets are wilder than models assume; he did not claim crashes are forecastable, and neither do we.

  OBJECTIVE   Direct quantitative crash modelling
  SOURCE      Sornette, Why Stock Markets Crash (2003); Johansen, Ledoit & Sornette, 'Crashes as Critical Points', Int. J. Theor. Appl. Finance 3(2), 219-255 (2000)
  CONTRIBUTES Bubbles as positive-feedback regimes with super-exponential price growth approaching a critical point.
  PRIMITIVE   SUPEREXP
  LIMIT       We implement the growth-acceleration diagnostic ONLY. We do not fit the log-periodic term or solve for the critical time t_c: Bree, Challet & Peirano (Quantitative Finance 13(2), 2013) show t_c is a 'sloppy' parameter, poorly constrained by the data, and that published confidence windows for it are 'likely to be over-optimistic'.

  OBJECTIVE   Implementation / rigorous econophysics
  SOURCE      Bouchaud & Potters, Theory of Financial Risk and Derivative Pricing (2nd ed., 2003)
  CONTRIBUTES Random-matrix cleaning of correlation matrices, tail estimation, and honest treatment of estimation error.
  PRIMITIVE   COUPLING
  LIMIT       Correlation structure is estimable only up to the Marchenko-Pastur bound at a given N and T. We report how many eigenvalues clear that bound rather than pretending the whole matrix is signal.

  OBJECTIVE   Empirical crisis antecedents  [ADDED - see note]
  SOURCE      Schularick & Taylor, 'Credit Booms Gone Bust', American Economic Review 102(2), 1029-61 (2012); Jorda-Schularick-Taylor Macrohistory Database (free)
  CONTRIBUTES Across 14 advanced economies, 1870-2008, credit expansion is the most informative measurable antecedent of financial crises.
  PRIMITIVE   FUNDING
  LIMIT       Operates at a macro/annual horizon. It tells you the system is loaded, never when it releases.
